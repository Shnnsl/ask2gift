# Amazon Associates Readiness Checklist

This checklist is meant to help Ask2Gift prepare responsibly for a future Amazon Associates application. Completing it does not guarantee approval.

## Site and content readiness

- Public website is live
- Custom domain is active
- Navigation works clearly on desktop and mobile
- Pages contain original, useful content
- Legal and disclosure pages are published

## Affiliate readiness

- Affiliate disclosure is clearly available
- Official tracking ID is added only after approval
- No fake clicks, incentives, or misleading claims are used
- No copied Amazon reviews, ratings, or product claims are shown
- No manually maintained Amazon price promises are presented
- No unauthorized Amazon branding is used

## Product-link quality

- Outbound gift links are tested
- External destination behavior is clear to users
- Mobile experience is checked
- Production URLs are accurate
- Tracking is verified only after approval and real tag setup
- Future catalog refinement should prioritize replacing generic search URLs with verified product or retailer destination links
