# Vision

## Problem

People often know they want to buy a gift but struggle to decide what would actually feel thoughtful, useful, and appropriate.

## Mission

Help people find the right gift.

## Product Vision

Ask2Gift aims to become a trusted gift discovery and recommendation engine that helps users make better decisions before they buy.

## Gift Engine Philosophy

The product should feel more like a thoughtful advisor than a generic product list. Recommendation quality, relevance, confidence, and variety matter more than simply matching loose tags.

## Guiding Principles

- Recommendation quality first
- Trust over hype
- Relevance over volume
- Thoughtful variety over repetition
- Simplicity over clutter

## Long-Term Direction

Ask2Gift is intended to become the place people visit before deciding what to buy and where to purchase it. Long-term growth can include broader catalog depth, stronger feedback loops, optional accounts, and responsible affiliate integrations.

## What Ask2Gift Is Not

Ask2Gift is not trying to become another general online store. It is not designed to overwhelm users with endless product listings or to prioritize commission over relevance.
